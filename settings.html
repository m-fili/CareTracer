How your identity is protected when external AI is used

Before any question is sent to an external AI service, CareTracer transforms it on your device:

  • Names and identifiers are replaced with stable code-words — for example, “John Doe” becomes “PT-7Q2”. The same person or lab always maps to the same code-word, so the Companion can still follow how things change over time.
  • Every date is shifted by a hidden offset that changes each session, so your real appointment, birth, and test dates never leave your device.
  • Each question is handled either entirely on your device or entirely by the external service — never both, and never combined.
Sent to external AI:  “Why did PT-7Q2's A1c improve after 2024-XX-XX?”

You can switch back to on-device-only at any time, and switching takes effect on your next question.
